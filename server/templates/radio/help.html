CardsEach record as a card, its fields along one line. Off: a table, one row per record, under column headings. On a phone the list is always cards.
ParagraphsIn the table: long values wrap onto more lines instead of ending in …. On cards: a line per field. Tables and cards each keep their own choice.
LinesOn to begin with. Whenever the list shows cards (Cards pressed, or a narrow screen), each field sits on one line of its own, names never broken, long values ending in …, all of it on hover. Press it off for Cards' fields flowing along a line (or, on a phone, values that wrap).
PanelShows or hides the side panel, on the left of the list whether the page is at the usual width or full width: what the search matched, in which fields, as badges with counts. Search starts with it open, the other lists closed.
Smaller or larger text, 100% to 200%, shown between them; the reset button goes back to 100%.
WidthThe whole page at the usual width (about 1200px, centred) or the full width of the screen.

Every list numbers its records from 1 (the # column, or the corner of a card); a search within a tab numbers what is left again from 1.

Text size, width and the panel are remembered in this browser for each page. Cards, Paragraphs and Lines start fresh each time a page opens (Lines on).

Member or public user

SearchOpens Search in a new tab, the log on stays where it is.
  • What was picked shows as badges ({{ ui.WHO.member.symbol }} member, {{ ui.WHO.public.symbol }} public, {{ ui.FIELD_SYMBOLS.vesselName }} vessel). on a badge removes it; removing the vessel keeps the member.
  • Boxes filled from a record are read-only (Mobile stays editable). If the record is wrong, fix it on its tab: the Member or Public vessel tab shows that record and saves in place.
  • A Member No. that was heard but is not a member goes into Notes; the member box only takes a real member.

The boxes

  • chooses a date, sets the time to now. How they logged on: Radio, Phone or In person.
  • A red box has to be fixed before the log on can go on the watch. An orange box is worth asking for and blocks nothing.
  • Save keeps it; once nothing needed is missing, saving puts it on the watch.
  • Discard draft (with a reason) for a call that was never a log on. Log off ends the watch when they are back.
  • History: every change to the log on, who made it and when.

👤 Members and public vessels

  • A member gets their number (m00001 and on) when saved. A mobile is 10 digits and shows as 0412 345 678.
  • A member's tabs: Details, Emergency contacts, Vessels, Trailers, Cars and History. Each tab is a list with the view buttons and its own search; Add opens a new one, opens one.
  • History on a member shows every change: their details and every contact, vessel, trailer and car, each marked with what it changed.
  • A public vessel holds the vessel, its owner's name, phone and email, notes, emergency contacts and history.
